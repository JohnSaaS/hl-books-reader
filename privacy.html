Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026.

Herrmann and Loll Books Reader is an internal tool run by Herrmann & Loll, a CPA firm. This page explains what it does with your QuickBooks data.

What We Access

When you connect your QuickBooks Online company, the tool can read your accounting records: accounts, transactions, customers, vendors, and reports. We use it to read your books. The tool is set up so it does not create, change, or delete anything in your QuickBooks company.

Why We Access It

Only to do the accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and tax work you have engaged the firm to do. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything unrelated to that work.

Who Can See It

Firm staff working on your account. The data is handled with the same confidentiality the firm applies to all client records under its professional obligations as a CPA firm.

How Long We Keep It

We keep what we read for as long as it is needed for your engagement and for the record retention periods that apply to accounting and tax work.

How to Stop It

You can disconnect the tool at any time from your QuickBooks Online settings, or ask the firm to disconnect it for you. Once disconnected, the tool can no longer read anything new from your company.
